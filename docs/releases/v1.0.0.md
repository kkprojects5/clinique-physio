First complete version of the Clinique Physio Mont-Royal system (D3). Student capstone project
(Vanier College, AEC Software Development), built by KK. **The clinic, its staff and all data
are fictional. Payments run in Stripe test mode only.**

## Try it

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| Website (patients, physiotherapists, administration) | https://clinique-physio.vercel.app |
| API and its reference | https://clinique-physio-api.onrender.com/docs |
| Front-desk app (Windows) | `CliniquePhysio-Accueil-Setup-1.0.0.exe` below |
| Patient app (Android) | `CliniquePhysio-1.0.0.apk` below |

Demo accounts are given to the supervisor privately. The free API sleeps when nobody uses it:
the first page can take about a minute.

### Install the front-desk app (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

1. Download `CliniquePhysio-Accueil-Setup-1.0.0.exe` and open it.
2. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**: click
   **More info → Run anyway**. No administrator rights are needed; .NET does not need to be
   installed.
3. Choose the language, then **Install**. The app opens on the login page (front-desk account
   and its two-step code).

Without installing: `CliniquePhysio-Accueil-1.0.0-portable.zip`, unzip, run
`CliniquePhysio.Desktop.exe`.

### Install the patient app (Android)

1. On the phone, download `CliniquePhysio-1.0.0.apk`.
2. Allow the browser to install unknown apps when Android asks, then **Install**.
3. Log in with a patient account. On a computer, the same APK installs in the Android emulator
   by dragging it onto the emulator window.

## What it does

- **Visitors:** services and prices, team with photos, free times, a chatbot for practical
  questions (it refuses medical advice and sends emergencies to 911, 811, 9-8-8), privacy
  policy in French and English.
- **Patients (website and Android):** register with consent, book and pay the deposit online,
  reschedule or cancel (24-hour rule), exercise program with check-off and pain level,
  insurance receipts (PDF), **Mes données** (consents, access, correction, deletion).
- **Front desk (Windows):** day schedule of all physiotherapists, booking by phone,
  check-in and no-show, balance paid at the desk (cash, debit, credit), receipts.
- **Physiotherapists (website):** working hours and time off, their patients, encrypted SOAP
  notes, signature and amendments, exercise programs and adherence chart.
- **Administration (website):** services and staff, audit log, weekly report, Law 25 requests,
  incident register.
- **Security:** two-step login for staff, roles and care-relationship checks, encrypted
  clinical data, brute-force protection, audit log that cannot be changed.

Scope: all 33 **Must** stories and 9 of 14 **Should** stories (64%) are done. The 9 stories
left out of v1.0.0 are listed with their reasons in
[docs/requirements.md](https://github.com/kkprojects5/clinique-physio/blob/main/docs/requirements.md).

## Known limitations

- No email or SMS provider: emails are prepared but not delivered; no reminders.
- The desktop app needs the API (no offline mode).
- The installer and the APK are not signed for a store (installed by hand).
- The free hosting sleeps when idle and keeps a limited database history.
