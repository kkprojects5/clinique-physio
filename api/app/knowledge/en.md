# Chatbot knowledge base — English

Fictional clinic (student project). Each section has an id in brackets, the same in both
languages. The "Keywords" line helps the search; it is never shown.
These texts contain no medical advice: the chatbot answers practical questions only.

## [hours] Opening hours
Keywords: hours, open, opening, closed, closing, when, evening, saturday, sunday, weekend
The clinic is open Monday to Thursday from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Friday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. It is closed on weekends and statutory holidays.
Online booking is available 24 hours a day on the website.

## [location] Address and directions
Keywords: address, where is it, where are you, located, find, metro, subway, bus, transit, get there, street, Saint-Denis
The clinic is at 4200 Saint-Denis Street, Montréal (fictional address), on the ground floor.
The nearest metro station is Mont-Royal (orange line), about a 5-minute walk.

## [parking] Parking
Keywords: parking, park, car, drive, bike, bicycle
There is no private parking. Paid spaces are on the street and in the municipal parking lot on Rivard Street. Bike racks are in front of the clinic.

## [accessibility] Accessibility
Keywords: accessible, accessibility, wheelchair, walker, crutches, elevator, stairs, steps, reduced mobility
The clinic is on the ground floor, with no steps, an automatic door and wheelchair-accessible washrooms. Tell the front desk if you need help when you arrive.

## [booking] Booking an appointment
Keywords: book, booking, make an appointment, appointment, schedule, online, phone, availability, slot
You can book online 24 hours a day: choose a service, a physiotherapist and a free time, then pay the deposit. You can also call the front desk during opening hours.
A chosen time is held for 10 minutes during payment.

## [first-visit] First visit
Keywords: first, new patient, assessment, what happens, how does it go, start, begin, first time
A new patient always starts with a 60-minute initial evaluation. The following appointments are follow-up treatments.
Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in the intake form.

## [what-to-bring] What to bring
Keywords: bring, wear, clothes, clothing, shorts, documents, report, imaging, x-ray, prescription
Wear comfortable clothes that let you move (shorts or loose pants). Bring your medical or imaging reports if you have any, your list of medications and your private insurance card.

## [referral] Prescription or referral
Keywords: prescription, referral, doctor, physician, need, required, without
In Québec, you can see a physiotherapist without a doctor's prescription or referral. Some private insurers require one to reimburse you: check your policy.

## [services-prices] Services and prices
Keywords: price, prices, cost, costs, fee, fees, charge, how much, services, evaluation, follow-up, rehabilitation, sports, surgery, length, minutes
Initial evaluation (60 min): $120. Follow-up treatment 30 min: $80. Follow-up treatment 45 min: $95. Sports rehabilitation (45 min): $100. Post-surgery rehabilitation (60 min): $120.
The "Services" page of the website has the current list with the physiotherapists for each service.

## [deposit] Online deposit
Keywords: deposit, pay, payment, card, online, Stripe, how much, advance
An online booking requires a deposit of $20 to $30 depending on the service, paid by card. The rest is paid at the clinic after the appointment. The appointment is confirmed when the payment is accepted.
An appointment booked by phone is paid in full at the clinic.

## [payment-methods] Payment methods at the clinic
Keywords: pay, payment, cash, money, debit, credit, card, Interac, invoice
At the clinic, you can pay by debit card, credit card or cash.

## [cancellation] Cancelling an appointment
Keywords: cancel, cancellation, refund, refunded, 24 hours, policy, fee, cannot come
You can cancel in "My appointments" on the website. More than 24 hours before the appointment, the deposit is refunded automatically to your card. Less than 24 hours before, the deposit is kept; to change an appointment that close, call the front desk.

## [reschedule] Changing the time of an appointment
Keywords: change, move, modify, reschedule, postpone, another time, another day
More than 24 hours before, you can move your appointment yourself in "My appointments", at no cost, to another free time. Less than 24 hours before, call the front desk.

## [no-show] Missed appointment and lateness
Keywords: missed, miss, forgot, forget, no-show, late, lateness, did not come
If you do not come to your appointment, the deposit is kept. If you are late, the session ends at the planned time so the next patient is not delayed. Let the front desk know as soon as possible.

## [insurance] Insurance and receipts
Keywords: insurance, insurer, reimburse, reimbursement, receipt, invoice, RAMQ, medicare, private, CNESST, SAAQ, covered
Physiotherapy in a private clinic is not covered by the RAMQ. Most private insurers reimburse part of it: you receive a receipt to send to your insurer. The clinic does not bill insurers directly.

## [languages] Languages
Keywords: language, languages, english, french, français, arabic, vietnamese, russian, speak
Care is offered in French and English. Some physiotherapists also speak Arabic, Vietnamese or Russian: the "Team" page shows each one's languages.

## [children] Children and teenagers
Keywords: child, children, my child, bring my kid, bring my child, kid, kids, son, daughter, baby, teenager, minor, age, parent, accompany, years old
Patients under 14 must come with a parent or guardian, who gives consent to care.

## [exercises-app] Home exercise program
Keywords: exercises, program, home, app, mobile, check off, follow, phone
Your physiotherapist may give you an exercise program. You see it in the clinic's mobile app, where you check off each session you do. For any question about an exercise, ask your physiotherapist.

## [account] Patient account
Keywords: account, sign up, register, log in, login, password, forgot, email, profile
Create an account on the website with your email, then confirm it with the link you receive. If you forgot your password, use "Forgot password?" on the login page.
I have no access to your account or your appointments: log in to see them.

## [privacy] Privacy
Keywords: privacy, data, personal information, confidential, Law 25, file, record, security, share
The clinic protects your personal information under Québec's Law 25. Clinical notes are encrypted and only the physiotherapists who treat you can read them. The privacy policy is on the website.
Do not share health information in this chat.

Keywords: contact, reach, phone, call, email, number, talk, human, person, receptionist
During opening hours, call the front desk at 514-555-0100 (fictional number) or write to info@clinique.example.com.
